## 1. Product and priorities

This repository contains a private, mobile-first HYROX training PWA. The main product areas are:

- workout library and workout editor,
- generated multi-week training programs,
- calendar and workout rescheduling,
- active workout runner with timers and splits,
- workout history and result entry,
- import of workout definitions,
- account and cloud synchronization.
